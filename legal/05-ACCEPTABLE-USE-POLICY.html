1. WHO THIS POLICY APPLIES TO
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1.1  This Acceptable Use Policy ("AUP") applies to every User and
     Contributor of the Platform. By using the Platform you accept this
     AUP. It supplements (and is part of) the Terms and Conditions in
     legal/01-TERMS-AND-CONDITIONS.txt.

2. WHAT THE PLATFORM IS FOR
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  The Platform exists for one purpose: free sharing of legitimate study
     material (class notes, unit notes, practical records guidance, previous
     year question papers and similar educational material) with students,
     in English and Hindi.

3. PROHIBITED CONTENT — NEVER UPLOAD
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  No Contributor shall upload, and no User shall attempt to introduce,
     any of the following ("Prohibited Content"):

     (a)  COPYRIGHT INFRINGEMENT: any material the Contributor does not own
          or hold rights/licence to publish — including scanned or
          photo-copied commercial textbooks, guides, coaching-institute
          packets, paid test series, and material taken from other
          websites, apps or persons without written permission;

     (b)  LEAKED OR CONFIDENTIAL EXAM MATERIAL: unreleased question papers,
          answer keys, internal assessment documents or any material whose
          disclosure is an offence;

     (c)  OBSCENE MATERIAL: anything obscene or lascivious within the
          meaning of Section 67 of the Information Technology Act, 2000;

     (d)  DEFAMATORY OR ABUSIVE MATERIAL: statements harming the reputation
          of any person (including students, teachers or staff), abusive,
          harassing, threatening or hateful content targeting any
          individual or community (including content offensive on grounds
          of religion, race, caste, sex, gender identity or nationality);

     (e)  HARMFUL CODE: malware, viruses, scripts, exploits or any file
          designed to harm devices or deceive Users;

     (f)  PERSONAL DATA OF OTHERS: phone numbers, addresses, marks, photos
          or any private information of any person without that person's
          consent — including "fun" or "expose" pages;

     (g)  IMPERSONATION: any material that misrepresents the uploader as
          another person, teacher or authority;

     (h)  POLITICAL / FUNDRAISING / COMMERCIAL PROMOTION: advertisements,
          paid promotions, fee-collecting forms, betting links, donation
          drives or any commercial material;

     (i)  ANYTHING UNLAWFUL: any material the publication of which
          constitutes an offence under any law in force in India.

4. UPLOADER RESPONSIBILITIES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  A Contributor is the publisher of their own Content. Before uploading,
     they must confirm that:
     (a)  they authored the material themselves, or have the explicit
          permission of every co-author / rights-holder;
     (b)  the material is genuinely course-related study material;
     (c)  the file does not contain Prohibited Content;
     (d)  the file name and language label (English / Hindi) are correct,
          so students are not misled.

4.2  By uploading, Contributors grant the licence and give the warranties
     set out in legal/07-CONTENT-CONTRIBUTION-LICENSE.txt.

5. PROHIBITED USE OF THE PLATFORM ITSELF
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  No person shall:
     (a)  attempt to gain unauthorised access to the repository, the
          hosting, or any account/token of another person;
     (b)  overload, flood, scrape or disrupt the Platform or GitHub's
          services;
     (c)  copy the entire Platform (code, design, name) and republish it as
          another project in a manner that misleads Users;
     (d)  use the Platform's name or the Owner's identity to solicit
          students for money, tuitions or anything else without written
          permission.

6. ENFORCEMENT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6.1  The Owner may, without notice and without prejudice to other remedies:
     (a)  remove or disable any Content that violates this AUP;
     (b)  decline future contributions from a violating Contributor and
          remove them from the "Contributed Teachers" list;
     (c)  preserve evidence and report serious violations (exam leaks,
          obscene material, harassment) to the college authorities and/or
          law-enforcement agencies, including through the National Cyber
          Crime Reporting Portal (cybercrime.gov.in).

6.2  Violations may also attract penalties under the Information Technology
     Act, 2000 (including Sections 43, 66, 66C, 66D and 67), the Copyright
     Act, 1957 (Sections 51 and 63) and the Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita, 2023.

8. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
