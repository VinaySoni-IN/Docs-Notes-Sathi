2. WHAT YOU CAN COMPLAIN ABOUT
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2.1  Any problem with the Platform or its Content, including:
     (a)  Copyright infringement;
     (b)  Privacy concerns (including anything concerning a minor);
     (c)  Defamatory, abusive, obscene or hateful material;
     (d)  Suspicion of leaked / confidential exam material;
     (e)  Factual errors in notes;
     (f)  Broken links, wrong subject/semester classification, file
          problems;
     (g)  Any other unlawful or improper content or conduct.

3. TIMELINES (BINDING COMMITMENTS)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3.1  Acknowledgment of receipt         : within 48 hours
3.2  Interim action where the material is prima facie unlawful
     (e.g. removal/disablement of the file)             : within 72 hours
3.3  Final, reasoned decision on the grievance           : within 15 days
3.4  Matters involving the safety of children or exam integrity receive
     PRIORITY handling — action is attempted the same day.

4. PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
4.1  Step 1 — File the FIRST COMPLAINT REPORT (Part B below) through any
     official channel.
4.2  Step 2 — You receive an acknowledgment with a reference number.
4.3  Step 3 — The Grievance Officer examines the complaint, may seek
     clarifications or the concerned Contributor's response, and takes
     action (removal, correction, rejection of complaint with reasons).
4.4  Step 4 — You are informed of the outcome in writing.
4.5  Step 5 — If you are not satisfied, you may escalate (Part C).

5. RECORDS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5.1  Complaints received via GitHub Issues are inherently time-stamped and
     public. WhatsApp complaints are retained on the Officer's phone solely
     for handling the grievance, and deleted once the retention need ends.

================================================================================
PART C — ESCALATION IF UNSATISFIED
================================================================================

1.  If the Grievance Officer's decision does not satisfy you, or the
     timelines are not met, you may:

     (a)  Request a review by the Owner (same channels) with the reference
          number of your complaint;

     (b)  Approach the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal:
          https://cybercrime.gov.in
          (or the cyber-crime helpline 1930 for online fraud);

     (c)  Approach the competent court of law at Balaghat, Madhya Pradesh,
          or any other forum with lawful jurisdiction;

     (d)  For copyright matters, follow the full procedure in
          legal/03-COPYRIGHT-POLICY-AND-TAKEDOWN-NOTICE.txt, including the
          counter-notice route.

2.  Nothing in this document obliges you to exhaust these channels before
     approaching a court or authority where the law otherwise permits.
