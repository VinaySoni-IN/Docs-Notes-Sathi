Notes Sathi is a free educational notes platform for students. These notices reflect the legal information published in the project repository.

Notes Sathi is provided as-is without warranties of any kind. Notes are contributed by teachers and contributors for study purposes only. The content is not an official publication of any school, college, university, or examination body. Always verify information with the official syllabus, textbooks, and teachers.

Accuracy and liability

The owner, contributors, and publishers are not liable for errors, omissions, inaccuracies, or consequences resulting from use of the content. Notes are a study aid, not an official source.

Privacy

Notes Sathi does not collect, track, store, or sell personal data. There are no accounts, ads, or analytics. Theme, semester, bookmarks, and reading history are stored only in the student’s own browser and do not leave the device. The app reads notes from a public GitHub repository.

Notes are shared for personal study only and may not be sold or redistributed without the respective author’s permission.
