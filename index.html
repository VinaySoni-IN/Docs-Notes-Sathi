The useful parts

Everything students need to find the next note.

A lightweight, mobile-first classroom workflow: publish once, keep notes organised, and make every upload reachable without a login or server.

01

Upload dashboard

Teachers sign in once with a fine-grained GitHub token, choose program, branch, semester and subject, then publish PDFs from inside the app.

02

Drag, tag, publish

Drop English and हिंदी PDFs, detect language pairs automatically, track progress, and safely overwrite existing files when needed.

03

Stories + NEW tags

Uploads from the last 24 hours get a NEW label; story rings keep recent material visible for up to 72 hours.

04

Read PDFs here

Use the embedded PDF viewer for pages, pinch-zoom, download, and open-in-tab reading on any device.

A calmer study rhythm

From upload to revision in a few clear taps.

Teachers can publish directly from the in-app Upload tab: sign in once with a fine-grained GitHub token, choose program, branch, semester and subject, then drag and drop PDFs. The dashboard handles language tagging, upload progress, existing-file updates, and the repository commit; students see the new material through stories and NEW badges. The token stays in the teacher’s browser and should be cleared on shared devices.
